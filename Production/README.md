# Production files

`gerbers.zip` is the complete nine-file PCB export generated by KiCad 10.0.7 from the rectangular PCB source. It contains front/back copper, front/back solder mask, front/back silkscreen, Edge.Cuts, and separate PTH/NPTH Excellon drills. It is copied byte-for-byte from the previously validated review package, not assembled from the earlier incomplete five-file export.

Board design: 125 x 110 mm, two copper layers, nominal 1.6 mm thickness. Confirm copper weight, finish, board color and fabrication settings at checkout; those are not all encoded in Gerbers.

Native validation reports are in `Reports/`. Their ignored-check categories remain visible in the reports. A clean configured DRC is not a product-safety certificate or proof of physical fit.

Case production outputs: see `../CAD/README.md`. The supplied enclosure/keycap STEP exports are retained as sources, not silently relabeled as complete printable/assembled models. Do not send an unverified conversion to a printer simply because its extension is STEP.

The owner reports checking CAD fit. No independent physical overnight, power-fault, or alarm-loudness testing has been recorded. Only use a checked low-voltage USB supply and inspect/measure the first assembled board before unattended operation.
