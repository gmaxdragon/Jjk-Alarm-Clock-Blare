# Production files

## PCB

`gerbers.zip` is the complete nine-file export generated by KiCad 10.0.7 from the current rectangular PCB. It contains front/back copper, front/back solder mask, front/back silkscreen, Edge.Cuts, and separate PTH/NPTH Excellon drills. It is copied byte-for-byte from the validated export, not assembled from the earlier incomplete five-file set.

Board: **125 x 110 mm**, two copper layers, nominal **1.6 mm** thickness. Copper weight, finish and fabrication options still need to match the order. Native reports are in `Reports/`; their configured ignored checks remain visible. A DRC pass is not a hardware-safety certificate.

## Keycaps

`Case_Parts/Extended_Keycap_01.stl` through `Extended_Keycap_04.stl` are individual watertight exports recovered from the owner's keycap STEP. The corresponding four-solid STEP is `../CAD/Recovered/Keycaps_Recovered.step`.

Each part is approximately **18.10 x 18.097 x 62.351 mm**. Coordinates are preserved from the source, so a slicer may need to center each part on its bed. Do not rescale. Retain the internal socket cavities. There are six switches and four supplied cap bodies; print two additional copies after verifying the switch-stem fit on one sample. The geometric checks do not establish mechanical fit or print orientation.

## Enclosure

`../CAD/Recovered/Enclosure_REFERENCE_ONLY.stl` is provided to recover access to the owner's existing geometry. It is deliberately outside this production folder because open seams remain. It is not an approved printable case or a complete assembled clock. Details and source hashes are in `../CAD/Checks/Export_Recovery.json`.

## Review scope

The owner reports checking fit in CAD. The current package is for design review, not a claim of an already assembled, tested clock. No independent physical power-fault, overnight, or alarm-loudness testing has been recorded. The buzzer remains disabled by the firmware's first-power configuration until its electrical commissioning is complete.
